Netmasha Attribution

Connects this Odoo shop to your Netmasha account: it keeps the Netmasha click id of a shopper on the sale order, and reports paid orders and refunds to Netmasha in real time.

What it does

  • Keeps the attribution. A shopper who arrives from the Netmasha mall carries an opaque click id. The addon stores it on the sale order, so Netmasha knows which orders came from the mall.
  • Reports paid orders. When a customer invoice of such an order becomes paid, the addon sends a signed event to Netmasha.
  • Reports refunds. When a credit note is posted for such an order, the addon sends a signed refund event to Netmasha.
  • Never blocks your shop. Every event is signed with a secret only your shop and Netmasha know. If a delivery fails, the addon writes a warning in the log and moves on; Netmasha's hourly readback reports anything that was missed.

Setup in 4 steps

  1. Install the module from Apps and make sure the Netmasha website tracking code is on your shop pages (it is part of the module).
  2. Open Settings → Netmasha.
  3. Paste your Netmasha store id and your webhook secret, then save. The secret is write-only: it is never shown again, so leave the field empty to keep the saved one.
  4. Keep Send events in real time on, and place a test order. Netmasha receives the paid order as soon as the invoice is paid.

What leaves this shop

Only the following is sent to Netmasha, and only for orders that carry a Netmasha click id:

  • For a paid order: the sale order id and name, the total and the tax amount, the currency, the number of order lines, the order date and the opaque click id.
  • For a credit note: the credit note id, the id of the sale order it belongs to, the amount, the currency and the invoice date.

Privacy

The addon stores one thing on your side: the opaque Netmasha click id on the sale order. It is a random reference, not a customer record. No customer name, address, e-mail or payment detail is stored by the addon and no customer data leaves your Odoo server: the order totals listed above are the only information that is sent to Netmasha.

Requirements

  • Odoo 17.0 with the Sales, Invoicing and Website modules.
  • A Netmasha account and a webhook secret from your Netmasha store page.
