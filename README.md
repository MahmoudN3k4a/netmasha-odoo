# Netmasha Attribution for Odoo 17

Odoo addon that links sales in your Odoo store to the Netmasha shoppers who sent them. It keeps Netmasha's click id on website orders, reports paid orders, cancellations, refunds and credit notes to Netmasha (signed with your webhook secret), and lets shoppers spend Netmasha points as single-use discount codes through Odoo's Discount & Loyalty (`sale_loyalty`).

## Install

1. Copy `netmasha_attribution/` into your Odoo `addons_path` (or install it from Odoo Apps).
2. Restart Odoo, update the Apps list and install **Netmasha Attribution**.
3. Open Settings, find **Netmasha** and press **Connect to Netmasha**. Sign in on Netmasha, agree, and you come back connected. Nothing to copy or paste.
